[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_SE | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The mission is to produce future software designers and innovators who will shape the future of the nation through
high‑quality education, research, and technopreneurship. The program provides students with state‑of‑the‑art knowledge
of the field and develops their practical skills to meet current industrial requirements, while enhancing their ability
to adapt to new developments in tomorrow’s technology.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_SE | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The program prepares students to:
1. Critically apply concepts, theories, and practices to provide creative solutions to complex computing problems.
2. Effectively communicate their ideas in written and electronic form and work collaboratively in a team environment.
3. Build a strong theoretical background in software engineering concepts and train on applied research of the field,
   needed to secure a doctorate position in the future.
4. Join dynamic and diverse career positions in computing‑related fields and maintain a growing career in a rapidly
   evolving field.
5. Apply theories and practices to provide creative solutions to complex computing problems.
6. Respond to current and emerging industrial needs by utilizing modern trends for building complex software systems.

[Program Learning Outcomes]
Program Code: MS_SE | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
1. Have the ability to design systems, components, or processes to meet desired needs within realistic constraints
   such as economic, environmental, social, political, ethical, health and safety, manufacturability, and sustainability.
2. Understand the impact of engineering solutions in a global, economic, environmental, and societal context.
3. Use the techniques, skills, and modern software engineering tools necessary for developing reliable software.
4. Demonstrate an understanding of the applied current theories, models, and techniques that provide a basis
   for the software lifecycle.
5. Communicate effectively with different audiences.
6. Contribute to improving the world through research and development in areas such as healthcare, transportation,
   education, etc.

[Deficiency / Prerequisite Courses]
Program Code: MS_SE | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The following core undergraduate‑level courses (or equivalents) are recommended to be completed before entering
the MS (Software Engineering) program:
- Object Oriented Programming
- Data Structures
- Software Engineering
- Software Design and Analysis
- Software Quality Engineering
Additional notes:
- A student selected for admission having deficiencies in the above courses may be required to study a maximum
  of four deficiency courses.
- These deficiency courses must be passed in the first two semesters.
- Deficiency courses are determined by the Graduate Studies Committee before admitting the student.
- Fee is not charged for studying deficiency courses.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_SE | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Master of Science (Software Engineering) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.50.
