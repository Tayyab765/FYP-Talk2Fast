graduates to create meaningful value for business and society in both local and global contexts.

- Marketing
- Human resource management

- Data analytics

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MBA | Program Title: Master of Business Administration | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences | Program Title: Master of Business Administration | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
For the award of Master of Business Administration degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 60 credit hours (through exemptions and/or by studying a course), including
  all those courses which have been specified as core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.50.

[Notes]
Program Code: MBA | Program Title: Master of Business Administration | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences | Program Title: Master of Business Administration | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
- Note 1: Registration in Dissertation‑I / MBA Project requires **30 credits** (including exemptions and the
  course of Advanced Research Methods – ARM) and a **CGPA of 2.50 or more**.
- Note 2: Dissertation of **6 credit hours** will be done by an individual student.
- Note 3: Courses marked with * may be exempted for graduates with business degrees. To qualify for the block
  exemption, a candidate must have studied at least:
  - Three courses each in management, quantitative stream, and English/business communication.
  - Two courses each in accounting, finance, and marketing.
  - One course each in economics, human resource management, and information technology.
  The MBA admission committee may allow up to two exemptions from the above requirements on a case‑by‑case basis.
