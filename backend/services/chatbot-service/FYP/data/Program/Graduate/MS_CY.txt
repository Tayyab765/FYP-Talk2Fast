[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_CY | Program Title: Master of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The mission of the MS (Cyber Security) program is to develop knowledgeable professionals with the skills to detect,
respond to, and prevent cyber‑attacks in organizations and protect national security. With a graduate degree in
cyber security, students build a comprehensive and strong foundation in the technical skills needed to keep
organizational computer systems, networks, and data safe, secure, and private. The program is designed to prepare
students with both theoretical and practical knowledge and to enhance their research skills in these emerging areas.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_CY | Program Title: Master of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
This program prepares students to meet challenges posed by a highly interconnected world. Graduates can work
in roles related to mobile computing, sensor networks, and other emerging technologies that require a thorough
understanding of related technologies and the latest trends in cyber security.

[Program Learning Outcomes]
Program Code: MS_CY | Program Title: Master of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
1. Equip students so that they are able to play an effective role in international efforts to make cyberspace
   safe, secure, and reliable for the national and international community.
2. Prepare students to learn the technical aspects of information security by understanding current threats
   and vulnerabilities and examining ways of developing effective countermeasures.
3. Produce skilled professionals who are able to contribute towards protecting national information infrastructure
   from all kinds of threats.
4. Prepare students who can understand the implications of handling data in terms of data security and
   business/organizational ethics, and who can make effective oral and written presentations on technical topics.

- Deficiency courses must typically be passed in the first two semesters.
- Fee is not charged for studying deficiency courses.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_CY | Program Title: Master of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Master of Science (Cyber Security) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.50.
