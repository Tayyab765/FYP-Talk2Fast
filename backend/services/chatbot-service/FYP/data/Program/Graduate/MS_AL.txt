[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_AL | Program Title: Master of Science (Applied Linguistics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Sciences and Humanities
There is an ever‑growing demand for trained English teachers. Colleges, universities, and language schools all
need qualified English teachers. English has now become more than a mere language; it is a bridge across borders
and cultures, and a source of unity in a rapidly evolving world.
This program enables students to learn how linguistic analysis can be used in practice in the vast arena of
language with specializations in:
- Computer Assisted Language Learning (CALL)
- Computational Linguistics (CL)
- English Language Teaching (ELT)
- English for Specific Purposes (ESP)
The program inculcates specialized skills to effectively teach the English language and integrates language and
computing skills.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_AL | Program Title: Master of Science (Applied Linguistics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Sciences and Humanities
- To produce qualified teaching resources for English language teaching at colleges, universities, and language schools.
- To integrate ICT (Information and Communication Technologies) in the teaching of English language.
- To develop collaborative learning resources for teaching English.
- To encourage teachers to be critically engaged in advanced research in English language teaching and learning.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_AL | Program Title: Master of Science (Applied Linguistics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Sciences and Humanities
For the award of Master of Science (Applied Linguistics) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including all those courses which have been specified as
  core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.50.
