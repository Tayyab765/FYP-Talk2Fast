[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_Civil | Program Title: Master of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
To develop highly competent graduates with advanced engineering knowledge and research skills required for
professional excellence and socio‑economic development.

[Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)]
Program Code: MS_Civil | Program Title: Master of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
The MS (Civil Engineering) program aims to:
- Enable graduates to successfully engage in multidisciplinary civil engineering practice to contribute to
  socio‑economic development.
- Develop the ability to lead a team as well as to be a successful team member for lifelong learning and
  career development.
- Enable graduates to contribute to civil engineering through research and innovation.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_Civil | Program Title: Master of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
For the award of Master of Science (Civil Engineering) degree, a student must have:
- Completed 30 credit hours as per the approved study plan; and
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.50.

[Credit Structure]
Program Code: MS_Civil | Program Title: Master of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
The program may be completed as either a research‑based or a project‑based option (subject to approval of the
Departmental Graduate Studies Committee – DGSC):
Research‑Based Option:
- Core Courses: 3 courses, 9 credit hours
- Elective (Specialization) Courses: 4 courses, 12 credit hours
- Elective Course: 1 course, 3 credit hours
- CV5091 MS Thesis‑I*: 1 course, 3 credit hours
- CV5092 MS Thesis‑II: 1 course, 3 credit hours
- Total: 30 credit hours

[Credit Structure]
Program Code: MS_Civil | Program Title: Master of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
Project‑Based Option**:
- Core Courses: 4 courses, 12 credit hours
- Elective (Specialization) Courses: 5 courses, 15 credit hours
