[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_SPM | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Project Management) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
To develop students' skills necessary to manage Software Project Management, coupling both theoretical and practical
aspects, so that they can acquire leadership positions in the software industry and become agents of change and
improvement.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_SPM | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Project Management) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The program prepares students to:
1. Assess and manage risks by developing strategies to handle them.
2. Gather and manage requirements and provide effective coordination between relevant stakeholders.
3. Identify, document, and analyze change management to agree on changes to scope.
4. Perform configuration management of large‑scale software.
5. Identify, prioritize, and agree on software releases by preparing release schedules and estimating software costs
   and resource requirements.
6. Identify and resolve conflicts that may arise during the management of software projects.
7. Inculcate a sense of ethics, responsibilities, and leadership in managing software projects.

[Program Learning Outcomes]
Program Code: MS_SPM | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Project Management) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
1. Students will be able to exhibit expertise in managing large‑scale projects as their final year project or conduct
   research related to Software Project Management.
2. Students will demonstrate analytical and communication skills while excelling in project resource forecasting
   and quality implementation for successful completion of projects.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_SPM | Program Title: Master of Science (Software Project Management) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Master of Science (Software Project Management) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.50.
