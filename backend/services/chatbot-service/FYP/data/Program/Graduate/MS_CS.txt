[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_CS | Program Title: Master of Science (Computer Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The Master of Science (Computer Science) coursework is tailored according to international standards to nurture
capacity building and original thinking in postgraduates for lifelong learning. The goal is to produce graduates
who are highly sought after by national and international IT organizations and who can pursue further studies
in national and international universities.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_CS | Program Title: Master of Science (Computer Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
Graduates of the MS (Computer Science) program are prepared to:
1. Become computer scientists who fulfil the requirements of the industry's software needs.
2. Help fill the vacuum created by a lack of computer science and technology education in the local region and beyond.
3. Develop a broad understanding of the computer science field by mastering innovative concepts, theories, and techniques.
4. Sharpen their communication skills and produce well‑organized technical reports.
5. Build strong formal foundations for further higher education (e.g., PhD).
6. Develop as highly skilled researchers and scholars who may pursue careers in the field of computer sciences.

[Program Learning Outcomes]
Program Code: MS_CS | Program Title: Master of Science (Computer Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
1. Students will possess advanced knowledge of the computer science field.
2. Students will be able to think creatively and critically to solve non‑trivial problems.
3. Students will be able to use computing knowledge to develop efficient solutions for real‑life problems.
4. Students will be able to design tailored solutions and conduct research‑related activities.
5. Students shall have the ability to make effective oral and written presentations on technical topics.

[Deficiency / Prerequisite Courses]
Program Code: MS_CS | Program Title: Master of Science (Computer Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The following core undergraduate‑level courses (or equivalents) are recommended to be completed before entering
the MS (Computer Science) program:
- Computer Programming
- Data Structures
- Operating Systems
- Database Systems
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Computer Networks
- Theory of Automata
- Software Engineering

[Deficiency / Prerequisite Courses]
Program Code: MS_CS | Program Title: Master of Science (Computer Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
Additional notes:
- A student selected for admission having deficiencies in the above courses may be required to study a maximum of
  four deficiency courses.
- These deficiency courses must be passed in the first two semesters.
- Deficiency courses are determined by the Graduate Studies Committee before admitting the student.
- Fee is not charged for studying deficiency courses.
- A student may be allowed to take Research Methods and Applied Programming along with deficiency courses.
- If a student has a deficiency in Theory of Automata, they cannot take Theory of Programming Languages and
  Advanced Theory of Computation until the deficiency is cleared.
- If a student has deficiencies in Programming Fundamentals, Object Oriented Programming, and Algorithms, these
  may be covered through the Applied Programming course.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_CS | Program Title: Master of Science (Computer Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Master of Science (Computer Science) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.50.
