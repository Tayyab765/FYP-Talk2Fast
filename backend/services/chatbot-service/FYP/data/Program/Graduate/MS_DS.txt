[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_DS | Program Title: Master of Science (Data Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The MS (Data Science) coursework is tailored according to international standards to nurture capacity building
and original thinking in postgraduates for lifelong learning. The goal is to produce graduates who are highly
sought after by national and international organizations and who can pursue further studies in national and
international universities.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_DS | Program Title: Master of Science (Data Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
This program equips students to transform data into actionable insights that enable complex business decisions.
Students will be able to process large and complex data sets through computational, statistical, and machine learning
techniques. The program provides exposure to the latest trends and technologies in data science, thus producing
the manpower to fuel national and international emerging markets of data science products.

[Program Learning Outcomes]
Program Code: MS_DS | Program Title: Master of Science (Data Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
1. Equip students to transform data into actionable insights to make complex business decisions.
2. Enable students to understand and analyze problems and arrive at computable, data‑driven solutions.
3. Expose students to technologies that match real‑world data science solutions.
4. Provide hands‑on experience with data‑centric tools for statistical analysis, visualization, and big‑data
   applications at a practical, rigorous scale.
5. Help students understand the implications of handling data in terms of data security and business ethics.
6. Develop students' ability to make effective oral and written presentations on technical data‑science topics.

[Deficiency / Prerequisite Courses]
Program Code: MS_DS | Program Title: Master of Science (Data Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
The following (or equivalent) undergraduate‑level courses are prerequisites for starting MS (Data Science) coursework:
- Object Oriented Programming
- Data Structures and Algorithms
Additional notes:
- A student selected for admission having deficiencies in the above courses may be required to study them.
- These deficiency courses must be passed in the first two semesters.
- Deficiency courses are determined by the Graduate Studies Committee before admitting the student.
- Fee is not charged for studying deficiency courses.
- A student may take the Statistics and Mathematical Data Science course along with the deficiency courses.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_DS | Program Title: Master of Science (Data Science) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Master of Science (Data Science) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.50.
