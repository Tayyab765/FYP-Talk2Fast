[Program Vision]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
To be a leading master's program in Business Analytics, producing experts who use data‑driven insights to make
innovative decisions and take meaningful action in a complex global business environment.

[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
The Master of Science (Business Analytics) program aims to equip graduates with advanced analytics tools,
statistical methods, and machine learning techniques to solve complex business problems. Emphasizing ethical,
data‑driven decision‑making, sustainability, and a culturally informed approach, the program inspires innovation,
entrepreneurial action, and sustainable business improvement, preparing business analysts who drive digital
transformation and create meaningful value for society.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Graduates of the Master of Science (Business Analytics) program can choose from roles such as:
- Business analytics consultant
- Operations analyst
- People (HR) analyst
- Financial analyst
- Market or consumer analyst
- Business intelligence analyst
- Analytics consultant
- Product or strategy analyst

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
The program prepares graduates with a computer science background to transition into strategic management roles
by gaining business knowledge, while enabling those with a business background to move into data‑driven
leadership positions by developing technical analytics skills.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
For the award of Master of Science (Business Analytics) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 43 credit hours.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.50.

[Credit Structure]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Total Program Credits: 43 credit hours.
Both business and non‑business background students follow a four‑semester structure with:
- Core analytics and methods courses (e.g., Advanced Data Analysis, Decision Science for Business).
- Supporting courses in programming, databases, and business foundations (depending on background).
- Electives in MG/AF/BA/CS.
- MS Project or MS Dissertation (two‑stage: Dissertation‑I and Dissertation‑II).

[Credit Structure]
Program Code: MS_BA | Program Title: Master of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Registration in 'MS Dissertation‑I' shall be allowed provided the student has:
- Earned at least 18 credit hours; and
- Passed the 'Research Methodology' course; and
- CGPA is equal to or more than 2.50.
