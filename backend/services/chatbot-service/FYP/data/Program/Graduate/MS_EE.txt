[Program Mission]
Program Code: MS_EE | Program Title: Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
The purpose of the Master of Science program in Electrical Engineering is to attain theoretical and practical
depth in one of the areas of interest. The MS (EE) program is structured to enhance students’ critical thinking
and intuitive abilities through a combination of highly specialized courses and expert supervision. The program
aims to produce graduates who have the abilities and skills to be employed as practicing engineers in fields
such as design, research and development, testing, and manufacturing, as well as to assume positions of leadership
and responsibility within organizations.

[Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)]
Program Code: MS_EE | Program Title: Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
The mission is to:
- Provide students with advanced learning and application in a discipline or sub‑discipline of Electrical Engineering.
- Teach tools and techniques required for advanced learning, research, and application in any discipline or
  sub‑discipline of Electrical Engineering.
- Enhance skills in areas such as problem‑solving, mathematical modelling, writing and oral presentation,
  leadership, understanding the interrelation of business with technology, and ethics applied to Electrical Engineering.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: MS_EE | Program Title: Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
For the award of Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 30 credit hours, including three core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.50.

[Credit Structure]
Program Code: MS_EE | Program Title: Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
The MS (EE) program comprises:
- Core Courses: at least 3 advanced EE core courses.
- Elective Courses: a set of CS/EE electives in the chosen area of specialization.
- Research Methodology: a mandatory core course for the thesis option.
- Thesis/Project: MS Thesis or MS Project (for thesis‑based option), as approved by the Departmental Graduate
  Studies Committee (DGSC), summing to a total of 30 credit hours.

[Study Plan]
Program Code: MS_EE | Program Title: Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
The typical study plan spans four semesters and includes:
- Completion of the required core courses.
- Completion of elective courses in the chosen specialization (e.g., controls, embedded systems, communications,
  power/energy, signal processing, networks, etc.).
- Completion of MS Thesis or MS Project as per the approved path, with total credit hours summing to 30.

[Registration Rules for Thesis / Project]
Program Code: MS_EE | Program Title: Master of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Graduate | Domain: Engineering
- Research Methodology (EE5011) is mandatory for MS with thesis.
- Registration in 'MS Thesis‑I / Project‑I' is allowed provided the student has:
  - Earned at least 15 credit hours; and
  - CGPA is equal to or more than 2.50.
- Subjected to the approval of Departmental Graduate Studies Committee (DGSC).
