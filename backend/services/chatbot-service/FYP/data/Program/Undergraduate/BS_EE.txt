[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_EE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
The mission of the School of Electrical Engineering is to:
- Develop a nurturing environment for delivering quality engineering education through discovery and innovation.
- Show commitment and dedication to teaching by developing state‑of‑the‑art curriculum grounded in scientific,
  mathematical, and technical knowledge that emphasizes practical application and provides opportunities for
  hands‑on experience.
- Inculcate lifelong learning and instill ethical and social values in graduates to help them acquire professional
  leadership.
- Act as a centre of excellence to identify problems of national industry and develop innovative solutions.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_EE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
This versatile degree opens careers in different areas of Electrical Engineering. Graduates can pursue careers in
the telecom industry, electronics, power sector, embedded system design, and software programming, as well as in
other industries that require strong electrical and computing skills.

[Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)]
Program Code: BS_EE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
- PEO‑1 – Creativity: Apply cutting‑edge engineering practices to develop sustainable solutions for complex
  engineering problems under limited resources.
- PEO‑2 – Conduct: Uphold responsible, professional, and ethical conduct with strong interpersonal skills.
- PEO‑3 – Leadership: Emerge as team leaders in their domain of expertise and in activities that support service
  and economic development, nationally and throughout the world.
- PEO‑4 – Versatility: Practice electrical engineering in a broad range of industries in a multicultural environment
  and adapt to embrace new technologies.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_EE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Electrical Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Electrical Engineering) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 140 credit hours, including all specified core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.
