[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_CE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Computer Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
To prepare competent Computer Engineering graduates, conscious of professional, ethical, and social responsibilities,
for productive engineering careers in industry, academia, and research, both locally and abroad.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_CE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Computer Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
This versatile degree opens careers in different areas of Electrical and Computer Engineering. Graduates can pursue
careers in the telecom industry, electronics, power sector, embedded system design, and software programming, among
other domains that require strong computing and engineering skills.

[Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)]
Program Code: BS_CE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Computer Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
The following Program Educational Objectives describe what the graduates of the Computer Engineering program are
expected to achieve within a few years after graduation:
- PEO1 – Creativity: Apply cutting‑edge engineering practices to develop sustainable solutions for complex engineering
  problems, considering the constraints of limited resources.
- PEO2 – Conduct: Uphold responsible, professional, and ethical conduct with strong interpersonal skills.
- PEO3 – Leadership: Emerge as team leaders in their domain of expertise and in activities that support service and
  economic development, nationally and throughout the world.
- PEO4 – Versatility: Practice computer engineering in a broad range of industries in a multicultural environment and
  adapt to embrace new technologies.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_CE | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Computer Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Computer Engineering) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 140 credit hours, including all specified core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.
