[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_AI | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Artificial Intelligence) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
To provide theoretical knowledge and practical skills required for a professional career in Artificial Intelligence.
The degree program follows Artificial Intelligence in breadth, covering core computing concepts with a specialization
in Artificial Intelligence and its application within the code of ethics.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_AI | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Artificial Intelligence) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
You may become a risk manager, reporting analyst, project manager, enterprise analyst, data engineer,
machine learning engineer, or Artificial Intelligence developer, etc.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_AI | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Artificial Intelligence) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Artificial Intelligence) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 137 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.00.
