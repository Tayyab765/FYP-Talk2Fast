[Program Vision]
Program Code: BS_BA | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
To be a leading undergraduate program in business analytics, empowering students with cutting‑edge analytical
skills, innovative thinking, and a strong ethical foundation to thrive in a dynamic, technology‑driven global economy.

[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_BA | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
The BS (Business Analytics) program aims to develop aspiring professionals with the expertise to drive
data‑informed decision‑making by integrating business management, technology, and analytics. The program
emphasizes ethical responsibility, sustainability, and the development of communication, analytical, and
problem‑solving skills to deliver lasting value to the global economy and the society.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_BA | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Graduates of the Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) program can select from several careers in analytics
in the areas of:
- Business
- Operations
- People (HR / workforce analytics)
- Finance
- Marketing
They can leverage their data analytics skills to translate structured and unstructured data into valuable
business insights by identifying patterns, trends, and anomalies that inform strategic decisions, optimize
operations, enhance customer experiences, and drive sustainable growth across the organization. The program
equips graduates to work in cross‑functional teams and provides a strong foundation for careers or further
studies in business and analytics.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_BA | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 135 credit hours, including all those courses which have been specified
  as core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.

[Credit Hour Distribution]
Program Code: BS_BA | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Business Analytics) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Eligibility for FYP‑I: 105 credit hours (CH) completed.
Domain‑wise Credit Hours:
- General Education: 34 credit hours
- Disciplinary Major: 80 credit hours
- Interdisciplinary: 12 credit hours
- Field Experience (Internship): 3 credit hours
- Capstone Project (FYP): 6 credit hours
- Total: 135 credit hours
