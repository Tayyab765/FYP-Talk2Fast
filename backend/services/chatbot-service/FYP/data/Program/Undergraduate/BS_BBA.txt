[Program Vision]
Program Code: BS_BBA | Program Title: Bachelor of Business Administration | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
To be a premier undergraduate program that develops graduates with strong analytical, professional, and ethical
business skills, who excel in the global job market and in their professional journeys.

[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_BBA | Program Title: Bachelor of Business Administration | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
The BBA program strives to deliver a rigorous curriculum that develops high‑achieving and aspiring management
professionals with the leadership, decision‑making, and analytical skills needed to make responsible contributions
for societal and economic development.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_BBA | Program Title: Bachelor of Business Administration | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Graduates of the Bachelor of Business Administration program can choose from a number of pathways as they enter
their professional life, including:
- General management
- Human resource management
- Finance
- Marketing
- Project management
- Supply chain management
- Business analytics
- Operations management
The program also develops the skills needed to become potential entrepreneurs and facilitates those with an
entrepreneurial inclination in planning for and launching their business ventures.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_BBA | Program Title: Bachelor of Business Administration | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
For the award of Bachelor of Business Administration degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 135 credit hours, including all those courses specified as core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.

[Credit Hour Distribution]
Program Code: BS_BBA | Program Title: Bachelor of Business Administration | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Eligibility for FYP‑I: 105 credit hours (CH) completed.
Domain‑wise Credit Hours:
- General Education: 34 credit hours
- Disciplinary Major: 80 credit hours
- Interdisciplinary: 12 credit hours
- Field Experience (Internship): 3 credit hours
- Capstone Project (FYP): 6 credit hours
- Total: 135 credit hours
