[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_CY | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
To provide theoretical knowledge and practical skills required for a professional career in computing and Cyber Security.
The degree program follows Cyber Security in breadth, covering core computing with a specialization in Cyber Security
and its application within a code of ethics.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_CY | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
You may become a Cyber Security specialist, Cyber Security analyst, cybercrime analyst, Cyber Security consultant,
IT auditor, information security manager/administrator, information assurance engineer, IT security consultant,
digital forensics analyst, penetration tester, or vulnerability tester, etc.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_CY | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Cyber Security) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 137 credit hours, including all core courses.
- Earned a CGPA of at least 2.00.

[Credit Hour Distribution]
Program Code: BS_CY | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Cyber Security) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Computing
Eligibility for FYP-I: 99 credit hours (CH) completed.
Domain-wise Credit Hours:
