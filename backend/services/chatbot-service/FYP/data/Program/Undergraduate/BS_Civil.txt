[Program Vision]
Program Code: BS_Civil | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
To achieve national and international recognition through innovation in Civil Engineering education, research,
and community services.

[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_Civil | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
To develop highly competent graduates with sound engineering knowledge, social responsibility, and lifelong
learning skills, enabling them to have successful careers in Civil Engineering professions. This mission is
achieved through:
- Providing a vibrant teaching and learning environment to deliver high‑quality engineering education.
- Maintaining a state‑of‑the‑art curriculum that emphasizes practical applications and provides opportunities
  for hands‑on experience.
- Developing an attitude for lifelong learning and instilling ethical and social values that help graduates acquire
  professional leadership.
- Identifying contemporary issues in the Civil Engineering profession and developing innovative solutions
  through research.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_Civil | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
A Civil Engineering degree prepares graduates for work in the construction industry as well as in relevant business,
management, and finance sectors. With a solid grasp of science, mathematics, and engineering knowledge, graduates
are able to design, create, and build sustainable civil infrastructure to serve society.

[Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)]
Program Code: BS_Civil | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
The objectives of the Civil Engineering program are to produce graduates who will:
- Successfully engage in contemporary Civil Engineering practice to solve real‑world problems.
- Pursue professional growth through continuous learning and experience.
- Maintain high ethical standards in the conduct of professional practice.
- Attain increasing levels of responsibility with strong interpersonal and communication skills.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_Civil | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Civil Engineering) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Engineering
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Civil Engineering) degree, a student must have:
- Earned at least 140 credits through respective core and elective courses offered in the study plan.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.
