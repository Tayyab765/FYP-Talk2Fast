[Program Vision]
Program Code: BS_FinTech | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
To be a leading program that nurtures globally competent professionals capable of driving innovation in the
financial technology sector by integrating financial expertise with cutting‑edge technological solutions.

[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_FinTech | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
The FinTech program seeks to provide a comprehensive blend of finance, technology, and entrepreneurial knowledge,
addressing the challenges posed by disruptive technologies in the financial services sector. With a focus on the
ethical, regulatory, and societal implications of FinTech, the program aims to empower students to design and
implement technology‑driven solutions that tackle complex financial challenges, improve business efficiency, and
contribute to sustainable economic growth.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_FinTech | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Graduates of the Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) program can pursue roles such as:
- Blockchain specialist
- Financial data analyst
- Digital payments expert
- Robo‑advisory associate
- Fintech consultant
Other career paths include positions like:
- Cryptocurrency manager
- RegTech analyst
- AI‑driven financial strategist
Opportunities from this degree span sectors such as digital lending, open banking, and financial cybersecurity.
The program prepares fintech professionals who can enhance operations, implement automation, and innovate using
data analytics, artificial intelligence, and blockchain technologies in the ever‑evolving financial sector.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_FinTech | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 138 credit hours, including all those courses which have been specified
  as core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.


[Credit Hour Distribution]
Program Code: BS_FinTech | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Financial Technology) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Eligibility for FYP‑I: 108 credit hours (CH) completed.
Domain‑wise Credit Hours:
- General Education: 34 credit hours

- Interdisciplinary: 12 credit hours
- Field Experience (Internship): 3 credit hours

- Total: 138 credit hours
