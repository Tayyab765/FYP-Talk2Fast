[Program Vision]
Program Code: BS_AF | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Accounting and Finance) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
To be a premier undergraduate program that develops graduates ready to excel in the dynamic local and global
financial landscape.

[Program Mission]
Program Code: BS_AF | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Accounting and Finance) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
The BS (Accounting and Finance) program has a mission to deliver education that integrates rigorous accounting
and financial knowledge with ethical decision‑making, technological aptitude, and critical thinking, empowering
graduates to meet the challenges of a complex and ever‑changing field of finance, with a focus on prudent,
ethical, and astute financial management practice.

[Career Opportunities]
Program Code: BS_AF | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Accounting and Finance) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Graduates of the BS (Accounting and Finance) program can opt for careers across accounting and finance, including:
- Audit
- Taxation
- Budget analysis
- Financial accounting
- Management accounting
The program also develops competencies for roles in:
- Financial analytics
- Corporate finance
- Professional accounting
- Investment banking
Additionally, graduates can pursue opportunities in financial institutions and consultancy firms, where their
specialized accounting and finance knowledge is in high demand. By combining a comprehensive curriculum with
hands‑on learning, the program prepares students with the knowledge and skills essential for success in the
dynamic accounting and finance sectors.

[Award of Degree]
Program Code: BS_AF | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Accounting and Finance) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
For the award of Bachelor of Science (Accounting and Finance) degree, a student must have:
- Passed courses totaling at least 138 credit hours, including all those courses which have been specified
  as core courses.
- Obtained a CGPA of at least 2.00.

[Credit Hour Distribution]
Program Code: BS_AF | Program Title: Bachelor of Science (Accounting and Finance) | Level: Undergraduate | Domain: Management Sciences
Eligibility for FYP‑I: 105 credit hours (CH) completed.
Domain‑wise Credit Hours:
- General Education: 34 credit hours
- Disciplinary Major: 83 credit hours
- Interdisciplinary: 12 credit hours
- Field Experience (Internship): 3 credit hours
- Capstone Project (FYP): 6 credit hours
- Total: 138 credit hours
