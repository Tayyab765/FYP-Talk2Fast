NU Online Admission Test (NU-OAT)

Information on Online Admission Tests conducted by the university is available on the University's admission website: admissions.nu.edu.pk

The online admission test is taken on a computer. The candidate should be familiar with the use of mouse and the keyboard. Each candidate is given a different examination. The sections of a test (English, Analytical skills, Math, etc.) appear in a random order. Moreover, questions related to different sections appear before the candidate in a random order. In each section, a candidate can move forward and backwards to review the responses selected. The candidate can change his or her response to any question within the current section.

Applicants should reach the test Centre on the date and time specified on the admit card. Latecomers will not be allowed to take the test. Applicants should not bring anything to the test Centre except their Admit Cards. No applicant will be allowed to take the test without an endorsed Admit Card. The Admission Test is held at all campuses of the University (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Multan and Chiniot-Faisalabad).

Topical Distribution of Admission Tests for Different Degree Programs

Admission Test Composition for BBA, BS(BA) & BS(A&F) Programs:
- Essay Writing: 15%
- Analytical Skills & IQ: 30%
- Basic Math: 55%

Admission Test Composition for BS (Computing) and BS (Engineering) Programs:
- Analytical Skills & IQ: 20%
- Basic Math: 30%
- Advanced Math: 50%
(Note: Essay Writing is not included in BS (Computing) and BS (Engineering) admission tests)

Admission Test Composition for MS Degree Program in Computing:
- Quantitative: 30%
- Analytical: 30%
- General Computing: 30%
- Essay Writing and Text Summary: 10%

General Computing Components (for MS Computing):
- Programming/Data Structure: 50%
- Operating Systems: 12.5%
- Networking: 12.5%
- Database Systems: 12.5%
- Software Engineering: 12.5%

Admission Test Composition for MS Degree Program in Engineering:
- Essay Writing: 10%
- Analytical skills & IQ: 20%
- Advanced Math: 10%
- Specialization Area: 40%
- Quantitative Methods: 20%

Admission Test Composition for MBA Programs:
- English: 10%
- Essay Writing: 30%
- Analytical skills & IQ: 20%
- Quantitative Methods: 20%
- Business Related knowledge: 20%

Preparation of Merit List

Merit Lists are prepared separately for each campus. For BBA and BS programs, 50% weight is assigned to marks obtained in the Admission Test (online NU Test or NTS exam), 40% weight is assigned to FSc (HSSC) or equivalent, 10% weight is assigned to Matriculation (SSC) or equivalent*.

* BS (Engineering) merit is calculated as per recommendation of PEC: 33% Admission test, 50% F. Sc (HSSC) and 17% Matric (SSC).

The University reserves the right to cancel the admission test of any candidate who is found involved in any illegal activity or for violating rules and regulations during the test.

Announcement of Selected Applicants

Selected candidates are informed through SMS on their cell phones and via email. The candidates should also check the status of their admission at the University websites: admissions.nu.edu.pk or www.nu.edu.pk using their ARN.

Cancellation of Admission

If a student is involved in any undesirable or illegal activity, then the University reserves the right to cancel the admission of that student. Moreover, the University reserves the right to cancel admission of any student at any time, if it is found that s/he has submitted false information/documents.
