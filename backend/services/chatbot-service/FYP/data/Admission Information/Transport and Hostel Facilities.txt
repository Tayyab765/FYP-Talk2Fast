Transport and Hostel Facilities - National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences
Transport & Hostel Facilities
The National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences provides transport and hostel facilities at various campuses to support students' accommodation and commuting needs.

Section: Transport Facility

Transport Facility - Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus
The campus provides a comfortable, safe and secure transport facility for the students, faculty, and staff. The University has arranged buses to operate between the campus and designated points in various localities of Faisalabad, Chiniot, and Chenab Nagar. This transport facility is available on a self-finance basis. The shuttle service is also operated in the afternoon for day scholars.

Transport Facility - Islamabad Campus
The University provides pick and drop transport services to students from designated routes in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Wah.

Transport Facility - Karachi Campus
The University arranges secure and safe, comfortable buses and coasters to pick students from selected points in different localities of Karachi. The transport facility is available on subsidized self-finance basis.

Transport Facility - Lahore Campus
The University provides a limited transport facility to pick and drop students from selected points in the city to the campus.


Transport Facility - Peshawar Campus
The University provides a safe and convenient pick and drop transport facility, on self-finance basis, along two routes from the main Peshawar city to the campus. On-campus hostel residents can avail pick and drop to/from Daewoo/Bus terminal on designated times on Friday and Monday.


Section: Hostel Facility

Hostel Facility - Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus
The campus provides on-campus separate hostels for boys and girls which accommodates more than 800 students and faculty. The hostels are fully furnished with state-of-the-art facilities like:
- Well-equipped Mess
- Study Room
- Food Storage (Freezer & Oven)
- Internet facility
- Iron facility
- Table Tennis
- Billiard Board
- Medical Centre
- Gymnasium & Fitness Centre

Hostel Facility - Peshawar Campus
The Peshawar campus provides an on-campus boys hostel with a capacity of 150 students. Each tenant is provided with own bed, bedside table, lockable cupboard, and study table. Other facilities include laundry, ironing, campus internet services, and weekly pick/drop to city-side bus terminals (Fixed time on Friday/Sunday, first come first serve). External campus managed hostels are also available on walking distance.
