How To Apply - National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES)
Section: Admission Procedure
The procedure for admission to National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES) involves several steps that must be completed online. The admission process is entirely digital and requires applicants to follow specific procedures.

Section: Online Application Process
The admission application form must be filled online by following the instructions provided on the university's admission portal. Applications received through post or by hand will not be accepted or entertained. The online system assigns a unique Application Reference Number (ARN) to each applicant, which must be noted for later use.

Section: Application Verification and Fees
Applicants must confirm that information entered in the admission application form is correct. In case of incorrect information, the university has all the rights to cancel the application or admission without any fee refund. Applicants must deposit the application fee in the designated bank, and the fee is non-refundable and non-adjustable.

Section: Admit Card and Testing
Upon payment of the application processing fee and additional admission test fee, the applicant will print their admit card bearing the applicant's test roll number. The applicant should then visit the test campus on the specified date and time as mentioned on the admit card. Admission of a student automatically stands cancelled if he or she fails in the required exam whose result was not available at the time of admission.


Section: Online Admission Test for Graduate Programs
Information on various admission tests is available on the university's admission website. The online admission test is taken on a computer, and candidates should be familiar with the use of mouse and keyboard. Each candidate is given a different examination, and the sections of a test (English, Analytical skills, Math etc) appear in a random sequence. Questions related to different topics also appear before the candidate in a random sequence.

Section: Test Format and Duration
In each section, a candidate can move forward and backwards to review the responses selected. The candidate can change their response to any question within the current section. Each section of the admission test has a fixed duration, and each section is timed independently. Time saved in one section is not carried forward to the next sections. At the end of the test, a summary of the results is displayed on screen. The duration of the online admission test is between two and three hours.

Section: Test Center Requirements
Applicants should reach the test center on the date and time specified on the admit card. Latecomers will not be allowed to take the test. Applicants should not bring anything to the test center except their admit cards. No applicant will be allowed to take the test without an endorsed admit card. The admission test is held at all campuses of the university including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Peshawar.

Section: Selection Criteria
Selection criteria information is available on the university's website for applicants to review the requirements and evaluation process.

Section: Selected Candidates Announcement
The selected candidates are informed through SMS on their cell phones and also via email. The candidates should check the status of their admission from the university website or the main university website.

Section: Foreign Nationals Requirements
At the time of admission, foreign nationals must submit a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Ministry of Education, Government of Pakistan. The procedure for admission for foreign nationals follows the same online application process as regular applicants.

Section: GRE and SAT Examinations
Those who have taken SAT examination can apply for admission to undergraduate programs, including BBA or BS programs. Those who have taken the GRE subject test can apply for admission to graduate programs, including MS, MBA, or PhD programs. The institution code for receiving SAT scores is 4575, and the institution code for receiving GRE scores is 7386.
