Admission Schedule 2025 - National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences
Updated on: May 19, 2025
Section: Admission Schedule 2025
The admission schedule for 2025 at National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences has been updated on May 19, 2025. The tentative admission schedule covers both undergraduate and graduate/post-graduate programs with specific dates for each phase.

---

Section: Application Submission Period
Admission application submission runs from May 19 (Monday) to July 4 (Friday) for both undergraduate programs and graduate/post-graduate programs. This is the initial application window where prospective students can submit their admission applications.

---

Section: Admission Testing Period
Admission tests are scheduled from July 7 (Monday) to July 18 (Friday) for both undergraduate programs and graduate/post-graduate programs. This two-week period allows for comprehensive testing of all applicants across different program levels.

---

Section: Merit List Announcement
The merit list announcement for selected and test-qualified candidates is scheduled for July 23 (Wednesday) for both undergraduate and graduate/post-graduate programs. This date marks when successful candidates are notified of their selection status.

---

Section: Graduate Program Interviews
Interviews for graduate programs are scheduled for July 25 (Friday). This interview process is specific to graduate and post-graduate programs only, as undergraduate programs do not require interviews.

---

Section: Post-Graduate Admission Tests
Additional admission tests specifically for post-graduate programs are scheduled for July 26 (Saturday). This is an additional testing requirement beyond the general admission tests, specifically for post-graduate level applicants.

---

Section: Admission Formalities
The admission formalities period runs from July 25 (Friday) to August 12 (Tuesday) for both undergraduate and graduate/post-graduate programs. This period allows selected candidates to complete their enrollment procedures and submit required documentation.

---

Section: Class Commencement
Classes commence on August 18 (Monday) for both undergraduate and graduate/post-graduate programs. This marks the beginning of the academic year for all admitted students across all program levels.
