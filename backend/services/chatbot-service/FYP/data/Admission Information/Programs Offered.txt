Programs Offered - National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES)
The National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES) offers undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate programs across multiple disciplines. The university operates six campuses: Chiniot-Faisalabad, Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Multan, and Peshawar.

---

Section: Undergraduate Programs
Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is available at Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, and Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Accounting and Finance (BS AF) is available at Islamabad Campus and Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence (BS AI) is available at all campuses: Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, Lahore Campus, Multan Campus, and Peshawar Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Business Analytics (BS BA) is available at Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, and Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering (BS CV) is available only at Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (BS CE) is available at Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, and Peshawar Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BS CS) is available at all campuses: Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, Lahore Campus, Multan Campus, and Peshawar Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Cyber Security (BS CY) is available at Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, and Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Data Science (BS DS) is available at Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, and Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (BS EE) is available at Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, Lahore Campus, and Peshawar Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Financial Technology (BS FinTech) is available at Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, and Lahore Campus.
Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BS SE) is available at all campuses: Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus, Islamabad Campus, Karachi Campus, Lahore Campus, Multan Campus, and Peshawar Campus.
