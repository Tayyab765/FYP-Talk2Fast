Academic Calendar for 2025-26 - National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences
Updated on: May 19, 2025
Section: Academic Calendar for 2025-26
The academic calendar for the 2025-26 academic year covers both Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 semesters with specific dates for registration, classes, examinations, and result announcements.

---

Section: Fall 2025 Semester Schedule
Registration for Fall 2025 semester runs from August 11 to 15 (Monday to Friday). This is the period when all students must complete their course registration for the fall semester.

---

Section: Fall 2025 - First Day of Classes
Classes for Fall 2025 semester commence on August 18 (Monday). This marks the official start of the fall academic semester for all programs.

---

Section: Fall 2025 - First Sessional Examinations
First sessional examinations for Fall 2025 are scheduled from September 20 to 23 (Saturday to Tuesday). These examinations assess student progress during the first part of the semester.

---

Section: Fall 2025 - Second Sessional Examination
Second sessional examinations for Fall 2025 are scheduled from November 1 to 4 (Saturday to Tuesday). These examinations assess student progress during the second part of the semester.

---

Section: Fall 2025 - Last Day of Classes
The last day of classes for Fall 2025 semester is December 5 (Friday). This marks the end of regular instruction for the fall semester.

---

Section: Fall 2025 - Final Examinations
Final examinations for Fall 2025 semester are scheduled from December 15 to January 2 (Monday to Friday). This extended period allows for comprehensive final assessments across all courses.

---

Section: Fall 2025 - Announcements of Results
Results for Fall 2025 semester will be announced on January 12 (Monday). This is when students receive their final grades and academic performance outcomes for the fall semester.

---

Section: Spring 2026 Semester Schedule
Registration for Spring 2026 semester runs from January 13 to 17 (Tuesday to Saturday). This is the period when all students must complete their course registration for the spring semester.

---

Section: Spring 2026 - First Day of Classes
Classes for Spring 2026 semester commence on January 19 (Monday). This marks the official start of the spring academic semester for all programs.

---

Section: Spring 2026 - First Sessional Examinations
First sessional examinations for Spring 2026 are scheduled from February 21 to 24 (Saturday to Tuesday). These examinations assess student progress during the first part of the semester.

---

Section: Spring 2026 - Second Sessional Examination
Second sessional examinations for Spring 2026 are scheduled from April 4 to 7 (Saturday to Tuesday). These examinations assess student progress during the second part of the semester.

---

Section: Spring 2026 - Last Day of Classes
The last day of classes for Spring 2026 semester is May 8 (Friday). This marks the end of regular instruction for the spring semester.

---

Section: Spring 2026 - Final Examinations
Final examinations for Spring 2026 semester are scheduled from May 18 to June 5 (Monday to Friday). This extended period allows for comprehensive final assessments across all courses.

---

Section: Spring 2026 - Announcements of Results
Results for Spring 2026 semester will be announced on June 8 (Monday). This is when students receive their final grades and academic performance outcomes for the spring semester.
